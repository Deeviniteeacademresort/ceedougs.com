Cee-Doug's Customer Care Guidelines

We are committed to providing a respectful, professional, transparent and customer-focused experience. Please follow these guidelines when communicating with our AI or human service representatives.

01

Provide Accurate Information

Always provide correct contact, delivery, order and destination information so our team can assist you efficiently.

02

Protect Your Personal Information

Never share passwords, PINs, card security codes or other sensitive security credentials through chat.

03

Treat Everyone With Respect

Our AI and human representatives are here to help. Please maintain respectful communication.

04

Keep Your Tracking Number Safe

Your tracking or order reference helps us locate your delivery and provide accurate assistance.

05

Allow Service Verification

For sensitive requests, Cee-Doug's may need to verify appropriate information before proceeding.

06

Escalate When Necessary

If AI assistance cannot resolve your request, ask for human support and your request can be placed in the service queue.
