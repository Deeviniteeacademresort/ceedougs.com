Cee-Dougs Foods & Commodities

From Nigerian farms and markets to homes and businesses.

Quality Nigerian Food.

TRUSTED COMMODITIES. BETTER VALUE.

Cee-Dougs Foods & Commodities is an operating business within the Cee-Dougs Group focused on the sourcing, processing, packaging and distribution of quality Nigerian food staples and agricultural commodities.

From grains and beans to flours, rice and other essential commodities, the business is built around a simple promise:

  • Quality you can trust
  • Value you can afford
  • Service you can rely on
  • Responsible sourcing
  • Dependable distribution
